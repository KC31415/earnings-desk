Verifier bugs found by reading the results. Reviewing every flagged claim turned up false alarms that were the verifier's fault, not the model's, and were fixed: sentences split across HTML blocks; quotes checked against the value field instead of the words in quotation marks; and cash outflows read as a sign error (which alone made April fiscal-year companies look worse). Each fix has a test, and the injected-error catch rate is re-checked after every fix so the verifier isn't loosened.

What the verifier can't check. Whether a qualitative claim (◦) is a fair reading of its paragraph, and whether the note leaves out something important. Those still need a human reader.
