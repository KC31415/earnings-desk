Snowflake is accelerating: revenue growth went from 28.7% to 35.1% over the last three quarters, and RPO is still up 30%. On a GAAP basis it is still deeply unprofitable (−17% operating margin), but the improvement is steady, helped by stock-based compensation falling from 35% to 27% of revenue. As a consumption business it benefits directly when customers run AI workloads on their data, which makes it more AI-exposed upside than AI-threatened. Net revenue retention of 126% is the number to track for whether that demand is durable.
