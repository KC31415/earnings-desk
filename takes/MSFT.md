Microsoft shows both sides of the AI trade. Revenue growth is steady at about 18%, and GAAP operating margin rose to 49.8%, but heavy AI infrastructure spending has pulled free cash flow margin down from 33% a year ago to about 20%. Commercial RPO of $677 billion, growing about 72% year over year, is a huge contracted backlog; how much of it is tied to a few AI customers is the question the filings alone can't answer. Watch whether capex intensity peaks before margins feel it.
