Nasdaq is included as a comparison: a market-infrastructure and fintech-software business whose results follow trading volumes and index flows more than the SaaS cycle. Revenue growth jumped from about 2–4% to roughly 21% in the last two quarters, with operating margin steady around 30%. Because its revenue is reported before transaction-based expenses, margins aren't directly comparable to the software names. Worth watching: the launch of 23/5 trading in December 2026, and whether index inflows keep driving the Solutions businesses.
