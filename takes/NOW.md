ServiceNow is the clearest counterexample to the SaaSpocalypse narrative among the apps in this set: revenue growth has stayed above 20% for five straight quarters and RPO growth is about 22%. The weak spot is GAAP profitability. Operating margin fell to 4.1% in Q2 before recovering to 9.4%, while stock-based compensation runs at 14–16% of revenue. Management is guiding current RPO growth down to 18.5% next quarter, so the question is whether AI products such as the $1.7 billion AI contract-value target offset a slowing core.
