Datadog's growth has accelerated for four straight quarters, from 28.1% to 35.6%, as customers building and running AI applications need more monitoring. It converts that into cash, with a 27% free cash flow margin, even though GAAP operating margin hovers around zero because stock-based compensation is about 20% of revenue. Backlog growth has cooled from over 50% to 43% and RPO slipped slightly quarter over quarter, so bookings are the thing to watch against the guided $1.135–1.145 billion next quarter.
