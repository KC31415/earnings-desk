Adobe was the poster child for "generative AI will disrupt creative software", yet year-over-year growth has risen from 10.7% to 12.9% over five quarters, with a GAAP operating margin of about 35% and free cash flow margins in the 30s. The caution sits in the backlog: RPO growth slowed from 13.1% to 8.4% in the latest quarter, and RPO and deferred revenue dipped sequentially. With a freemium push to more than one billion monthly users, watch whether paid conversion (ARR) keeps pace with usage.
