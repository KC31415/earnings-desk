The "AI eats SaaS" fear isn't visible in Salesforce's reported numbers yet. Revenue growth has held in a 9–13% band for five quarters (10.8% in FY2027 Q2), and remaining performance obligations are still growing about 11% a year. The bigger story is capital allocation: a $25 billion accelerated share repurchase and the Informatica, Contentful and Fin acquisitions mean reported growth increasingly mixes organic and acquired revenue. The test for next quarter is whether management's "second-half organic reacceleration" call shows up once acquisitions are stripped out.
