C3 AI is the counterpoint to "AI winners": an AI-native software vendor whose revenue fell 19–53% year over year for five straight quarters, to about $0.05 billion a quarter, with operating losses larger than revenue. Stock-based compensation has exceeded revenue in most recent quarters (113% in FY2027 Q1). Management says "the plan is working" after a sales reorganization, and free cash flow turned slightly positive on a large deferred-revenue inflow. Whether revenue stabilizes inside the $210–240 million full-year guide is the whole story.
